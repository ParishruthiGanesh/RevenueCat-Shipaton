# Demo video script (1:55)

Record the phone screen with Android's built-in **Screen record** (Quick Settings). Add the voiceover afterwards, or speak while you record. About 270 spoken words, at a calm pace.

**Before recording:**
- Sign in.
- Put a passport in a pouch in a drawer.
- Have a box with a few things in it.
- Have a second account already in your family.
- Turn off notifications (Do Not Disturb).

---

### 0:00–0:12 · Hook
**Screen:** Home screen. Then type "Where's my passport?"

> "My phone remembers every photo I've ever taken, and it still can't tell me where I put my passport. So I built Physical Memory."

### 0:12–0:35 · Remember it
**Screen:**
1. Tap the orange camera button.
2. Hold the mic and say "Remember where I'm putting my passport."
3. Take one photo of the passport going into the pouch in the drawer.
4. On the review screen, show the chain **Passport → Blue pouch → Top drawer → Dresser → Bedroom**.
5. Tap Remember.

> "I show it once. No tags, no typing. The AI works out the object and where it's going, a pouch, inside a drawer, inside a dresser, inside the bedroom. I confirm it in one tap."

### 0:35–0:55 · Ask it
**Screen:**
1. Go to the Ask tab and say "Where's my passport?"
2. Show the answer card and the confidence level.
3. Tap **Show me** and zoom into the photo.

> "Days later, I just ask. Notice the words: *last seen*. It never claims to know where my passport is. It tells me where it saw it, when, how sure it is, and shows me the photo as proof."

### 0:55–1:15 · Honest, not magic
**Screen:**
1. Take the pouch out of the drawer.
2. Hold to scan the drawer. Show the "Scanning · 12 frames" pill.
3. Show the **What changed** screen.
4. Ask again. Confidence drops to **Low**, with the reason.

> "Now I take the pouch out and sweep the drawer. It notices the pouch is gone, and the answer changes honestly: low confidence, and exactly why. Not seeing something isn't the same as proof it's gone."

### 1:15–1:35 · Pro + Family (RevenueCat)
**Screen:**
1. Go to You → Family. The paywall appears.
2. Tap **Start free trial** and complete the RevenueCat test purchase.
3. Create a family and show the invite code.
4. Cut to the second phone or browser asking "Where did Parishruthi put the charger?" and getting the answer with "added by…".

> "Pro is powered by RevenueCat: a free trial, then unlimited scans, cloud backup, and Family. One person pays, everyone else joins free with a code. Now my family can ask where I put things. And passports or private rooms never leave my account. The database enforces that, not just the app."

### 1:35–1:55 · Close
**Screen:**
1. Spaces tab: the map of rooms, furniture and boxes.
2. End card: app icon + "Physical Memory · Open source · github.com/ParishruthiGanesh/RevenueCat-Shipaton"

> "Every answer can be traced back to a photo. The AI sees; it never makes things up. I'm a student, and I built this solo: mobile app, database, AI and payments. Physical Memory: Google Search for the physical things in your life."

---

**Tips:**
- Rehearse once.
- Cut any wait longer than 2 seconds. The AI analysis takes a few seconds, so speed it up 2× in the editor.
- Upload to YouTube as **Public** or **Unlisted**, and paste the link into Devpost.
