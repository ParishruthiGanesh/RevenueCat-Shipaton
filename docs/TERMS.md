# Terms of use (draft)

*Draft for the Shipaton build. Have it reviewed before any public release.*

1. **What it is.** Physical Memory records where you last saw your belongings and helps you find them. It reports past observations with a confidence level. It can be wrong or out of date, and it doesn't guarantee that anything is where it was last seen.
2. **Not a medical device.** It's an organisational aid. It doesn't diagnose, treat or monitor any health condition.
3. **Your content.** You own your photos and memory. You give us permission to process them only to provide the service (for example, cloud analysis and backup). You can delete them at any time.
4. **Acceptable use.** Don't use the app to photograph or catalogue other people's belongings or private spaces without their permission.
5. **Subscriptions.** Pro is billed through the App Store or Google Play and renews automatically until cancelled in your store account settings. Prices and trials are shown before purchase. Cancelling never deletes your data.
6. **Liability.** The app is provided "as is", to the extent permitted by law.
